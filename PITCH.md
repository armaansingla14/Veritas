# Veritas Pitch Script (3 Minutes)

## Opening Hook (15 seconds)
"What if every citizen could access municipal services 24/7, in any language, with complete transparency? Meet Veritas - the civic clarity engine for Kingston."

## Problem (30 seconds)
- **311 Overload**: Municipal call centers handle 50,000+ calls yearly with long wait times
- **Language Barriers**: 15% of Kingston residents speak French as primary language; many more speak other languages
- **Accessibility Gaps**: Citizens with disabilities face barriers accessing civic information
- **Trust Deficit**: No way to verify government responses are accurate or unchanged
- **After-Hours Void**: City hall closes at 5pm, but problems don't wait

## Solution: Veritas (45 seconds)
"Veritas is an AI-powered civic clarity engine that transforms how residents interact with their city."

**Core Capabilities:**
1. **RAG-Powered Q&A** - Instant answers about bylaws, services, and procedures using Google Gemini
2. **Vision-Based Issue Detection** - Snap a photo of a pothole, graffiti, or broken streetlight - AI identifies and categorizes it
3. **Multilingual Voice** - ElevenLabs TTS reads answers aloud in English or French
4. **Blockchain Transparency** - Every response is cryptographically logged and anchored to Solana

## Live Demo (1 minute 15 seconds)

### Demo Flow:
1. **Ask Page** (20s)
   - "What are Kingston's noise bylaw hours?"
   - Show answer with citations
   - Click TTS button - hear the answer read aloud

2. **Scan Page** (20s)
   - Upload pothole photo
   - Watch AI detect issue type, severity, suggested department

3. **Report Page** (15s)
   - Quick 3-step report submission
   - Show blockchain verification badge on success

4. **Trust Dashboard** (10s)
   - Show hash chain visualization
   - Click Solana explorer link - prove immutable record

5. **ChatWidget** (10s)
   - "I want to report a pothole on Princess Street"
   - Show conversational report filing with TTS

## Tech Stack (20 seconds)
- **Gemini 2.0 Flash** - RAG generation + Vision scanning + Function calling
- **Solana Devnet** - Tamper-evident blockchain anchoring
- **ElevenLabs** - Natural multilingual TTS
- **Next.js 14** - Modern React framework
- **Tailwind + shadcn/ui** - Beautiful glass morphism design

## Impact & Feasibility (30 seconds)
**For Kingston:**
- Reduce 311 call volume by 30-40%
- 24/7 service availability
- Full accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Uses existing municipal knowledge base

**For Citizens:**
- Get answers in seconds, not minutes
- Report issues without phone calls
- Verify government isn't altering responses
- Access services in preferred language

## Closing (15 seconds)
"Veritas means truth. Every question, every report, every response - verified, transparent, and accessible. This is the future of civic engagement."
